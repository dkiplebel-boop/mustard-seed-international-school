Global Standards

As a fully accredited Cambridge International School, we offer a curriculum that is recognized and respected by the world's leading universities. From Early Years through to A-Levels, we implement the British National Curriculum with a local touch, ensuring our students gain a competitive edge in the global arena while maintaining deep roots in their own culture.

Holistic Growth

Education at Mustard Seed transcends the traditional classroom. We are committed to nurturing Emotional Intelligence (EQ) alongside academic IQ. Through our vibrant co-curricular programs—including music, debating, and community service—we build resilient leaders with the character, ethics, and social skills required to navigate the complexities of the 21st century.

Elite Facilities

Our school is a purpose-built ecosystem designed to inspire. We feature modern ICT laboratories, high-performance sports fields for athletic excellence, and quality science labs for hands-on discovery. Every corner of our facility is crafted to provide a safe, technologically advanced, and stimulating environment for modern learners.

Our History

21 Years of Excellence

Established over two decades ago in the serene suburbs of Kiamunyi, Nakuru, Mustard Seed began with a simple but powerful idea: that from a small seed of quality education, great leaders can grow.

What started as a local initiative has blossomed into a premier Cambridge International School. Over the years, we have expanded our facilities to include state-of-the-art labs, an aquatic center, and elite boarding wings, while maintaining the personal, family-oriented atmosphere that defines the "Mustard Seed Way."

IGCSE Grading System

The Cambridge Examination Board uses an **A*-G grading system** by which all work is assessed. Grades are awarded in the months following examination and coursework submission.

IGCSE & GCSE Differences

GCSE is compulsory within the UK, whereas the **IGCSE** is delivered to thousands of students worldwide. Both are highly-regarded variants of the UK National Curriculum and suitable precursors to A-level study. Click here for more information.

IGCSE Teachers

Our educators are highly experienced in delivering the syllabus and assessment criteria. We aim to provide growing young adults with the skills necessary to thrive in future studies and understand the world around them.

IGCSE Assessment

Criteria vary by subject: written exam papers, language oral examinations, or practicals for Drama and Science. Students may also present a coursework portfolio as part of the end-of-year review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mustard Seed a Day or Boarding School?

We offer both options. Our boarding facilities are available for students from Year 3 through Year 13.

What is the Teacher-to-Student Ratio?

We maintain a low ratio of 1:15 to ensure personalized attention and optimal learning outcomes.

Do you follow the Kenyan 8-4-4 or CBC curriculum?

No, we follow the British National Curriculum (Cambridge International Assessment).
